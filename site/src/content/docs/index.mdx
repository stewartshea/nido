*Nido* means "nest" — something you build, keep, and look after. Nido is a free,
open source tracker for the things that need looking after, and it starts with
the smallest and most relentless of them: a new baby.

Everything is yours to read, yours to run, and yours to keep. There is no paid
version, no account to create, and nothing leaves your home unless you ask it
to.

## What Nido does

A feed at 3am and two caregivers who both need to know what happened last. A
sleeper one of them is still awake for. The growth chart the paediatrician asks
about. All of it in one place, free, on hardware you control.

| Area | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Feeding** | Breast and formula timers, side and session tracking, amounts |
| **Diapers** | Wet, soiled, dry and combinations |
| **Sleep** | Start and stop, with live duration |
| **Growth** | Weight, height and head circumference against WHO and CDC percentiles |
| **Milestones & vaccinations** | Logging, plus a schedule of what is due |
| **Photos** | Per-member avatars and photo uploads |
| **Journal & mood** | Free-form notes for each day |
| **Shared access** | Invite other caregivers, with a role for what each one can do |
| **Reminders** | Nudges for stretches and intervals worth noticing |
| **Your data** | Export everything as JSON, any time, and move it back in |
| **Works offline** | Entries made without a connection wait, then send themselves |
| **Looks like yours** | Seven palettes in light and dark, plus a custom theme |

## What comes next

<div class="nido-card nido-card--edge">
	<p>
		A baby grows up, and the list of things needing care gets longer rather than
		shorter. Nido is being built to cover all of it — the same shared records,
		the same reminders, the same ownership, pointed at a whole home instead of
		one small person.
	</p>
</div>

The next tracker is **home maintenance and care**: the filter that needs
changing, the water heater that needs flushing, the smoke alarms that need
testing, the warranty that expires in March. Nido will keep these the way it
keeps a feeding — with history, with reminders, and shared between the people
who live with them.

It is being built in the open, alongside the infant tracker. If that is the part
you care about, it is written down in [the roadmap](./project/roadmap/).

Both are the same software with the same features, and neither has a paid tier.
Pick whichever fits your household.

## Help it keep going

Nido is maintained in one person's evenings. There is no company behind it, so
the two things that actually help are small ones:

- **[Contribute](./project/contributing/)** — a bug report with a reproduction,
  an answer to someone else's issue, or a note about your own setup.
- **[Sponsor the project](./support/)** — optional, unlocks nothing, and buys
  maintainer time: releases, security work, and answers.
