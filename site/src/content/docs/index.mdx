## What Nido does

<div class="nido-features">
	<div><strong>Feeding</strong><span>Breast, bottle and combo, with timers and amounts</span></div>
	<div><strong>Sleep</strong><span>Start and stop, with live duration</span></div>
	<div><strong>Diapers</strong><span>Wet, soiled, dry and combinations</span></div>
	<div><strong>Growth</strong><span>Weight, height and head against WHO and CDC curves</span></div>
	<div><strong>Shared</strong><span>Invite caregivers, with a role for each one</span></div>
	<div><strong>Yours</strong><span>Export everything, any time. Works offline</span></div>
</div>

Plus milestones, vaccinations, photos, a daily journal, and reminders — in
seven palettes, light and dark.

## What comes next

A baby grows up, and the list of things needing care gets longer. The next
tracker is **home maintenance and care** — filters, warranties, seasonal
upkeep — kept the same way Nido keeps a feeding, and already taking shape in
the app.

<figure class="nido-shot nido-shot--wide">
	<img src={homeSection.src} width="1440" height="1250" alt="The Home section of Nido: property assets, maintenance schedules, and manuals and routines" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

It is being built in the open, alongside the infant tracker — see
[the roadmap](./project/roadmap/).

## Help it keep going

Nido is maintained in one person's evenings. The two things that actually help
are small ones: [contribute](./project/contributing/) a bug report or an
answer, or [sponsor the project](./support/) — a monthly sponsorship, or
[buy the maintainer a coffee](https://ko-fi.com/sheastewart0494). Optional,
unlocks nothing, and buys maintainer time.
