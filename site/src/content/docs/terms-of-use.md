These terms cover **My Nido** ([my.nido-app.ca](https://my.nido-app.ca)), the
hosted version of Nido. If you self-host Nido, this page does not apply to you
at all — the software on your own hardware is yours, under the
[Apache-2.0 license](https://github.com/stewartshea/nido).

They are written to be read. The legal bits are at the bottom; the whole page
is short on purpose.

## The short version

- **My Nido is free and open source software.** The code is
  [Apache-2.0](https://github.com/stewartshea/nido). You are not paying for it,
  and nothing about your account depends on paying for anything.
- **Use it as you like.** Log what you want, invite who you want, export or
  delete anything any time. There is no fine print gating the features.
- **It is a service, not a guarantee.** One person runs it in their evenings,
  on hardware in Canada. It might be slow, it might be down, and one day it
  might go away. Plan accordingly.
- **Your data is yours, and your responsibility.** The operator keeps the
  lights on, but they are not responsible for your records. The durable copy
  lives with you — [export or back up](./getting-started/first-run/) your
  family's data regularly.
- **No warranty.** The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any
  kind, and so is the service built on it.

## What you get

An account and a place to record your family's life: feeds, sleep, growth,
inventory, reminders, and everything else the app does. Same code, same
features as a self-hosted install.

## What you're responsible for

- **Keeping your password safe.** The only thing between your family's records
  and the internet is that password.
- **Backing up what matters.** Backups and restores are supported in the app —
  [the backup guide](./guides/backup-and-restore/) shows the two halves, and the
  one to test once. Please do.
- **What you put in.** Photos, journal entries, and notes you upload are your
  content, and you're responsible for it — including that you're allowed to
  share it.

## What the operator is responsible for

Running the service honestly and openly: keeping the software in this
repository, storing your data in Canada, keeping the databases encrypted, and
sending only the emails [the privacy policy](./privacy/) lists.

That is the extent of it. There is no uptime guarantee, no data-loss guarantee,
no service-level agreement, and no priority support.

## Backing up is on you

My Nido keeps a copy of your data on its own disk, backed up on the operator's
schedule. That is convenient, but it is not the same as you owning a copy —
and it is not something you can rely on in an emergency. Use the built-in
export ([Backup & restore →](./guides/backup-and-restore/)) to download your
records from time to time. If your family's history matters to you, this is
the single most useful thing you can do.

## Services can end

My Nido is free, and it can go away. No account is promised forever. Before
that ever happens, there will be notice on this site and on
[the repository](https://github.com/stewartshea/nido) — and because exports
work any time, you will never be trapped.
