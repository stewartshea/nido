## What comes next

A baby grows up, and the list of things needing care gets longer. The next
tracker is **home maintenance and care** — filters, warranties, seasonal
upkeep — already taking shape in the app. It is being built in the open,
alongside the infant tracker. See [the roadmap](./project/roadmap/).

## Help it keep going

Nido is maintained in one person's evenings. Two small things actually help:

- [Contribute](./project/contributing/) a bug report or an answer
- [Sponsor the project](./support/) — monthly, or [a one-time tip on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/sheastewart0494)
