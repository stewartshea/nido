# Growth Tracking & Health Comparisons Specification

## Overview
Comprehensive growth tracking system that compares baby's development to public health standards from WHO and CDC.

## Core Growth Metrics
- **Weight Tracking**
  - Units: lbs/oz, kg/g with automatic conversion
  - Measurement timestamps
  - Growth velocity calculations
  - Percentile rankings vs standard curves
  
- **Height/Length Tracking**
  - Units: inches/cm with automatic conversion
  - Supine length (infants) vs standing height (toddlers)
  - Growth velocity tracking
  - Percentile comparisons
  
- **Head Circumference**
  - Measurement method tracking
  - Percentile analysis
  - Correlation with brain development

- **Body Mass Index (BMI)**
  - Calculation and tracking
  - Age-adjusted percentiles
  - Trend analysis

## Health Standard Integrations
- **WHO Growth Standards**
  - Breastfed infant growth charts (0-2 years)
  - Weight-for-age percentiles
  - Length/height-for-age curves
  - Weight-for-length percentiles
  - Head circumference charts

- **CDC Growth Charts**
  - US population-based standards
  - Extended age ranges (2-20 years)
  - BMI-for-age charts
  - Ethnicity considerations

## Advanced Analytics
- **Percentile Calculations**
  - Real-time percentile ranking
  - Growth velocity calculations
  - Crossing percentile lines detection
  - Early warning indicators

- **Pattern Recognition**
  - Growth acceleration/deceleration
  - Seasonal variations
  - Correlation with feeding/sleep patterns
  - Predictive modeling

- **Alert System**
  - Significant deviation warnings
  - Milestone delay alerts
  - Growth plateau notifications
  - Critical threshold breaches

## Data Visualization
- **Growth Curves**
  - Interactive percentile charts

  - Historical trend analysis
  - Comparative standard curves

- **Comparison Tools**
  - Side-by-side WHO/CDC comparisons

  - Developmental milestone tracking
