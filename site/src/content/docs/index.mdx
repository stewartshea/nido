## Two ways to run it

Same software, same features. Pick whichever fits your household.

<div class="nido-options">
	<div class="nido-option">
		<h3>Run it yourself</h3>
		<p>
			<strong>Free, on your own hardware.</strong> Five minutes with Docker
			and your records never leave the house. No account, no uploads, no one
			else involved.
		</p>
		<p><a href="./self-host/">How self-hosting works →</a></p>
	</div>
	<div class="nido-option">
		<h3>My Nido <span class="nido-option-tag">hosted in Canada</span></h3>
		<p>
			<strong>Free, for anyone who just wants to use it.</strong> Sign up with
			an email and a password and start logging tonight. Your data stays in
			Canada, and the only emails you get are about your account or the rules
			your family sets.
		</p>
		<p><a href="./my-nido/">About My Nido →</a> · <a href="./privacy/">Privacy policy →</a> · <a href="./terms-of-use/">Terms →</a></p>
	</div>
</div>

<p class="nido-webapp" role="note">
	<span class="nido-webapp__badge">Web app</span>
	Works right in your browser — nothing to install.
	On your phone, <strong>Add to Home Screen</strong> (iPhone) or
	<strong>Install</strong> (Android) for an app-sized shortcut.
</p>
